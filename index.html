Summary

Where simulation meets the physical world

Computational and experimental engineering researcher with 15+ years of experience in numerical simulation, fluid dynamics, multiphysics modeling, optimization, and data-driven research. Extensive expertise in CFD, CFD–DEM, CFD–TFM, multiphase and turbulent flows, granular materials, soft matter, EHD, micro-plasma actuators, aerodynamic flow control, and wind turbine and renewable-energy applications. Experienced in numerical algorithm development and improvement, parametric studies and optimization, HPC, experimental validation, optical and MRI-based imaging, and quantitative flow characterization. Complementary expertise in large-scale data analytics, machine learning, deep learning, and predictive modeling using Python/R.
